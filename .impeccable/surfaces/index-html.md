# Surface: Homepage (index.html)

Visitor mode: Experience. The work leads; the interface recedes.

## Audience and job

People who meet **Lee / leelab** through SalaryTicker, JobPilot, the CSDN blog, or GitHub; Windows workers evaluating the salary ticker; job seekers evaluating JobPilot; peers checking the craft. They should understand who this is, see both tools in a clear hierarchy, and be able to open a project, read the blog, inspect GitHub, or write an email.

## Action

Open SalaryTicker or JobPilot in a new tab; secondary actions are the blog, GitHub, and email.

## Proof and content

Two project cards: SalaryTicker is the full-width featured exhibit with its real app screenshot and labeled demo figures; JobPilot is the smaller companion with its real landing screenshot. A project index, 博客 / GitHub links, and email sit in the hero, About, and footer. No invented projects, user counts, press, or additional social links. JobPilot has no domain yet; keep 域名待定 / 在线预览.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A warm print-specimen personal homepage where one developer's small tools are mounted as two exhibits — a featured salary ticker and a companion job assistant — not a product microsite and not a row of equal portfolio cards. The person and the links lead; the tools follow.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper and sand grounds, warm-white plates, 1px warm borders, four-color registration lines, a personal four-color mark, a Song-style display serif, a system sans for reading, and mono for measurements. SalaryTicker is the featured saturated object; JobPilot is the smaller companion whose own violet screenshot supplies the product color. The eight-ray mark is the recurring ornament.

STORY: The visitor meets Lee, reads the promise 谋生有度，生活有光。, sees SalaryTicker with a live demo number and JobPilot with a real preview screenshot, and can open a project, read the blog, inspect GitHub, or write an email without friction.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Eyebrow and UTC+8 clock; an oversized two-line H1 with a drawn coral underline and a coral period; intro and primary CTA at lower left; 独立开发 · Windows 工具与 Web plus 博客 / GitHub links at lower right; the registered four-color rule; the Projects label and the top of the SalaryTicker card entering at the bottom right. The header/footer carry the personal mark, not the SalaryTicker app icon. A warm aurora and grain sit behind the paper, never over the type.

FORM: The brief pins an editorial poster plus two mounted specimen cards; the print-specimen direction and four-color registration system are position one in the derivation. seed: none (unavailable).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
